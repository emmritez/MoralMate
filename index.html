4-Step Classroom Rule for Asking Permission

STEP 1: Raise Hand

Never shout from your bench while the teacher is explaining.

STEP 2: Eye Contact

Wait until Ma'am or Sir nods at you before speaking.

STEP 3: "May I...?"

Use "Excuse me, May I..." instead of "Can I...".

STEP 4: "Thank You"

Always say "Thank you, Ma'am/Sir" after receiving a reply.
